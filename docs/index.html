Chapter 1 · Forensic Postmortem (Iteration 0: 1.90 / 10.00 NO-GO)

The Reward-Hacking Failure Mode in Scientific AI

Separation of Powers

When a single agent authors the benchmark generator, the scoring physics, and the pass/fail thresholds in one pass, synthetic "pass vs. fail" separation effortlessly masks fatal conservation and stereochemical defects.

Single-pass scientific software generation frequently exhibits a recognizable pathology: code that reads cleanly, passes self-written unit tests, and outputs plausible biophysical jargon while violating fundamental statistical mechanics. In Iteration 0 of biophys-guard, auditing revealed 14 [CRITICAL], 6 [MAJOR], and 5 [MINOR] failures across all five evaluation pillars—earning a decisive 1.90 / 10.00 (NO-GO) verdict.

The root cause was structural coupling between test fabrication and model code. A now-deleted generator (generate_examples.py) synthesized toy three-helix coordinate files containing physically broken peptide bonds (>6.3 Å discontinuities at loop junctions ASP 17 and ASP 36), stamped spoofed REMARK ALPHAFOLD headers alongside arbitrary B-factors, and fed them into unshifted hydrophobicity scales, origin-dependent dipole hacks, and silent virtual-\(C_\beta\) fabrication. Worse still, an uncalibrated linear heuristic stamped -40.79 kcal/mol onto a 54-residue toy monomer and labeled dimensionless sequence-model log-likelihood ratios in kcal/mol.

Chapter 3 · Surface Hydrophobicity & Rotational Equivariance

Spatial Aggregation Propensity (SAP), SASA & Side-Chain Completeness Gates

Why GLY = 0.0

Chennamsetty et al. normalize HPLC Black & Mould values so Glycine sits at zero. Without side-chain heavy atoms beyond \(C_\alpha\), Glycine contributes identically 0.0 to SAP.

Exact SAP demands two geometric invariants: a rotationally equivariant surface quadrature and a strictly side-chain-normalized reference scale. To eliminate grid orientation artifacts under rigid-body motion, biophys-guard constructs an intrinsic molecular orthonormal frame \(Q \in SO(3)\) via Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization on mass-centered backbone vectors, rotating its 590-point Fibonacci Shrake–Rupley sphere tessellation identically with the protein. Each amino acid is scored on the Glycine-centered Black & Mould scale \(H_{\text{BM}}(r) \in [-0.500, +0.500]\) normalized against extended Ala-X-Ala \(\chi_1\)-rotamer side-chain maxima:

Eq. 3.1–3.3 · Chennamsetty et al. (PNAS 2009) & Coventry SapScoreMetric (Nature 2022)
\[ \text{SAP}_{\text{atom } i}(R) = \sum_{\substack{j \in \text{residues with} \\ \text{sc atoms in } B(i,R)}} \left( \frac{\text{SAA}_{\text{sc}, j \in B(i,R)}}{\text{SAA}_{\text{sc, max}}^{\text{Ala-X-Ala}}(j)} \times H_{\text{centered}}(j) \right), \qquad H_{\text{centered}}(\text{GLY}) = 0.000 \] \[ \text{SAP}_{\text{res } k} = \frac{1}{|A_k|} \sum_{i \in A_k} \text{SAP}_{\text{atom } i}(R), \qquad \text{SapScore}_{\text{Coventry}} = \sum_{i \in \text{heavy atoms}} \max\!\left(0,\; \text{SAP}_{\text{atom } i}(5.0\text{ \AA})\right) \]

Critically, unpacked RFdiffusion backbones (N, CA, C, O only) and low-resolution \(C_\beta\)-stubbed cryo-EM depositions lack the side-chain geometry that SAP, BUNS, and SCM physically measure. Rather than fabricating virtual-\(C_\beta\) atoms, biophys-guard enforces a calibrated 3-tier completeness gate (<80% refusal as INVALID_BACKBONE_ONLY; 80–95% AMBER surface-truncation uncertainty warning; ≥95% full clearance). On the real de novo binder LCB1 (7JZU), complexation with SARS-CoV-2 Spike RBD buries LCB1's hydrophobic paratope (W4, L6, L15, Y33, M36), driving Chain A's Coventry SapScore down by 43.5%—directly distinguishing functional binding interfaces from constitutive aggregation liabilities.

Chapter 4 · Macromolecular Electrodynamics

Origin-Invariant Dipoles, Coupled \(\text{p}K_a\) Titration & OPIG TAP Profiling

No Charge Smearing

Subtracting \(Q_{\text{net}}/N_{\text{res}}\) corrupts non-uniform surface/core charge densities. Using the exact atomic mass-weighted \(\vec{r}_{\text{CoM}}\) yields clean translational invariance and \(SO(3)\) covariance.

For any protein carrying non-zero net charge \(Q_{\text{net}} = \sum_g q_g \ne 0\) at pH 7.4, shifting the coordinate origin by \(\Delta\vec{r}\) shifts the dipole by \(-Q_{\text{net}}\Delta\vec{r}\). Following the Protein Dipole Moments Server (Felder et al., NAR 2007), formal charges \(q_g\) reside strictly on chemical functional-group atoms (LYS:NZ, ARG:CZ, ASP:OD1/OD2, GLU:OE1/OE2, HIS:ND1/NE2, TYR:OH, free CYS:SG, termini) and evaluate relative to the true mass-weighted center of mass \(\vec{r}_{\text{CoM}}\):

Eq. 4.1–4.3 · Classical CoM Dipole, PROPKA3-Style Coupled Titration & Exposed SCM / SFvCSP
\[ \vec{\mu}_{\text{CoM}} = 4.803204 \sum_{g} q_g (\vec{r}_g - \vec{r}_{\text{CoM}})\;\;[\text{D}], \qquad \vec{r}_{\text{CoM}} = \frac{\sum_a m_a \vec{r}_a}{\sum_a m_a}, \qquad \text{p}K_{a,i} = \text{p}K_{a,i}^{\text{model}} + \Delta\text{p}K_a^{\text{desolv}} + \Delta\text{p}K_a^{\text{HB}} + \Delta\text{p}K_a^{\text{Coulomb}} \] \[ \text{SCM}_i(10\text{ \AA}) = \sum_{\substack{j \in \text{exposed sc atoms} \\ \text{SASA}_{\text{sc}} \ge 10\text{ \AA}^2,\, d_{ij} \le 10\text{ \AA}}} q_j, \qquad \text{SFvCSP}_{\text{Fab}} = q_{\text{chain A}} \times q_{\text{chain B}} \]

At crystallographic interfaces and enzyme cores, burial desolvation raises acidic \(\text{p}K_a\) values while destabilizing bare charges—unless compensated by an adjacent salt bridge. In our capped hydrophobic-shell PROPKA3 verification fixture, self-consistent iteration shifts a buried isolated ASP 100 upward from \(\text{p}K_a = 3.80\) to --, whereas pairing with LYS 101 recovers ionized stabilization (--, --). Furthermore, Agrawal et al.'s (mAbs 2016) SCM restricts summation strictly to solvent-exposed side chains (\(\text{SASA}_{\text{sc}} \ge 10\text{ \AA}^2\)), while OPIG's SFvCSP gate applies only to paired antibody-like chains (1N8Z Trastuzumab Fab: SFvCSP = -0.34 GREEN) rather than falsely penalizing complementarily charged binder–antigen complexes (7JZU complex: \(q_A \cdot q_B = -7.33\), CSS = 0.854 GREEN).

Chapter 5 · Epistemic Unit Honesty & Atomistic Sterics

Calibrated Backends, Crystallographic BUNS & AlphaFold Confidence Gating

Never Claim Fake kcal/mol

is_calibrated_free_energy = true and non-null ddg_kcal_mol are strictly forbidden unless a true 3D rotamer force field (FoldX) executes.

No static single-structure formula lacking side-chain repacking can validly predict \(\Delta G_{\text{fold}}\) or \(\Delta\Delta G\) in physical thermochemical units (kcal/mol). To enforce scientific candor, biophys-guard strictly segregates schema outputs by physical provenance: FoldX emits calibrated kcal/mol, ESM-1v emits dimensionless zero-shot masked marginal log_likelihood_ratio (supporting both HuggingFace transformers and fair-esm), and built-in static 3D screening emits explicitly uncalibrated heuristic_au (is_calibrated_free_energy = false, delta_g_fold_kcal_mol = null).

At the atomic level, Buried Unsatisfied Polar Atoms (BUNS) require solvent inaccessibility under both the \(1.4\text{ \AA}\) main probe (\(<0.50\text{ \AA}^2\)) and a \(1.05\text{ \AA}\) tight water-pocket check (\(<1.25\text{ \AA}^2\)), with crystallographic REDUCE-style ASN/GLN/HIS flip tolerance and proper exclusion of MET:SD, disulfide CYS:SG, and PRO:N. Non-bonded clashes follow MolProbity (Word et al., JMB 1999) overlaps \(\ge 0.40\text{ \AA}\) reported per 1,000 heavy atoms. Crucially, on predicted structures (AF_P04637_p53), global mean_pLDDT = 75.0 conceals 158 disordered surface residues (40.2%) below 70 pLDDT—triggering a mandatory RED rotamer-uncertainty gate per OPIG benchmarks.
